# Room 1 – The Idea Lab

## 1. Room Overview

The Idea Lab is the first Founder Assessment within **Escape Startup Lab**, designed to evaluate a team's ability to recognize entrepreneurial opportunities through evidence-based decision-making.

Candidate Teams assume the role of aspiring startup founders seeking investment from Startup Lab's Investment Committee. Before receiving funding, they must demonstrate that they can analyze business situations objectively and recommend an appropriate strategic initiative.

Inside The Idea Lab, players investigate a startup workspace left behind by previous founder candidates. The room contains customer interviews, operational reports, business performance data, marketing documents, brainstorming notes, and prototype concepts.

Each interactive object provides only one piece of the overall business situation.

No single clue reveals the best recommendation.

Instead, Candidate Teams must connect information from multiple sources, identify the underlying business problem, and justify their recommendation through evidence.

The room concludes with a **Founder Decision**, where the team submits one strategic proposal to the Investment Committee for evaluation.

The primary entrepreneurial competency assessed in this room is **Opportunity Recognition**.

---

## 2. Learning Objectives

Upon completing The Idea Lab, participants should be able to:

- Identify customer problems using multiple independent sources of evidence.
- Differentiate business symptoms from underlying root causes.
- Analyze customer, operational, and business information simultaneously.
- Recognize entrepreneurial opportunities based on validated evidence.
- Evaluate competing strategic alternatives before selecting a recommendation.
- Justify business recommendations using logical reasoning and supporting evidence.
- Collaborate effectively when making strategic business decisions under time constraints.

---

## 3. Story Context

After entering Startup Lab, Candidate Teams are welcomed by **EVA (Entrepreneurial Virtual Assistant)**, an artificial intelligence responsible for evaluating future startup founders.

EVA explains that many promising businesses fail because entrepreneurs become attached to ideas before validating customer problems.

To assess the team's entrepreneurial thinking, EVA grants access to **The Idea Lab**, an innovation workspace containing research conducted by previous founder candidates.

The room contains customer feedback, operational reports, business performance metrics, brainstorming materials, and unfinished innovation projects.

Some information is highly relevant.

Some information is merely contextual.

Candidate Teams must determine which evidence truly supports a business opportunity.

At the conclusion of the investigation, the team prepares a recommendation for Startup Lab's Investment Committee.

The committee is not searching for a "perfect" answer.

Instead, it evaluates whether Candidate Teams can justify their recommendation using reliable customer, operational, and business evidence.

Only recommendations supported by convincing evidence receive investment approval.

---

## 4. Room Layout

The Idea Lab resembles a modern startup innovation workspace where entrepreneurs research customer problems before developing products.

Players may freely investigate the room in any order.

Every interactive object appears equally important.

Candidate Teams must determine which information contributes meaningfully to the final recommendation and which information merely provides context.

The room contains three categories of interactive objects.

### Investigation Objects

Objects containing customer, operational, and business evidence that contribute to the team's understanding of the situation.

Each investigation object provides one unique insight.

No investigation object independently reveals the best recommendation.

### Environmental Objects

Objects that enrich the atmosphere and storytelling without directly contributing to the final Founder Decision.

These interactions provide immersion, humor, and personality while encouraging exploration.

### Founder Assessment Terminal

The Founder Assessment Terminal allows Candidate Teams to submit their Founder Decision to the Investment Committee.

The terminal is accessible throughout the investigation.

However, EVA reminds players that investors rarely approve recommendations supported by incomplete evidence.

```text
Explore Workspace
        │
        ▼
Collect Independent Evidence
        │
        ▼
Review Candidate Notebook
        │
        ▼
Identify Relationships Between Clues
        │
        ▼
Determine Root Business Problem
        │
        ▼

        │
        ▼
Founder Decision
        │
        ▼
Investment Committee Review
        │
        ▼

```

The challenge is not discovering one hidden clue.

The challenge is recognizing patterns across multiple independent sources of evidence.

## 10. Scoring System

The scoring system rewards entrepreneurial reasoning rather than simply interacting with objects.

Candidate Teams earn points by collecting diverse evidence, selecting the best-supported recommendation, and completing the assessment efficiently.

### Score Breakdown

| Category | Maximum Points |
|----------|---------------:|
| Customer Evidence Collected | 4 |
| Business Evidence Collected | 4 |
| Operational Evidence Collected | 4 |
| Founder Decision | 8 |
| Time Bonus | 5 |
| **Maximum Score** | **25 Points** |

### Founder Decision Evaluation

The Founder Decision is assessed using three criteria:

- Is the recommendation supported by customer evidence?
- Is the recommendation operationally feasible?
- Does the recommendation appropriately address the validated business problem?

Only the proposal that best satisfies these criteria receives full Founder Decision points.

### Time Bonus

| Remaining Time | Bonus Points |
|---------------|-------------:|
| More than 50% | +5 |
| 25–50% | +3 |
| Less than 25% | +1 |

The objective is to encourage efficient teamwork without sacrificing evidence quality.

## 12. Educational Feedback

Rather than simply indicating whether the team's proposal is correct or incorrect, EVA provides feedback from the perspective of the Investment Committee.

The objective is to reinforce entrepreneurial reasoning and evidence-based decision-making.

---

### Outstanding Performance

**Investment Committee Verdict**

> ✓ Proposal Approved

Excellent work.

Your recommendation demonstrates strong entrepreneurial thinking supported by customer, business, and operational evidence.

Rather than reacting to individual complaints, your team identified the underlying business problem and proposed a strategic initiative capable of creating meaningful customer value.

This is the type of reasoning investors expect from startup founders.

---

### Good Performance

**Investment Committee Verdict**

> ✓ Proposal Conditionally Approved

Your recommendation is reasonable and addresses an important business challenge.

However, stronger evidence or clearer justification would increase investor confidence.

Successful entrepreneurs validate assumptions before committing resources.

---

### Needs Improvement

**Investment Committee Verdict**

> ✗ Proposal Not Approved

Your recommendation is not sufficiently supported by the available evidence.

Entrepreneurs should avoid jumping directly to solutions before fully understanding the underlying customer problem.

Continue strengthening your investigation and analytical reasoning.

---

## EVA's Reflection

> Great entrepreneurs rarely succeed because they have the most innovative ideas.

> They succeed because they identify the right problems, validate them with evidence, and recommend solutions that create measurable value.

> Opportunity Recognition is the first step in every successful entrepreneurial journey.

This concludes **Room 1 – The Idea Lab** and prepares Candidate Teams for **Room 2 – The Customer Hub**, where they will learn that understanding **who** the customer is can be just as important as understanding **what** problem to solve.
